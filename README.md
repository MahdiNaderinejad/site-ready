# Site Ready

Safety training you play. 3 high risk trades, each on its own site, each a short
shift. You spot what is wrong and make the call. When the call is wrong the game
shows what happens next, then lets you rewind and do better.

**[Play it here](https://mahdinaderinejad.github.io/site-ready/)**

| Trade | Job | Site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welder | Hot Work. Cut a bracket off an air header | Wastewater plant deck at sunrise |
| Electrician | Dead Before Touch. Lock out and test a pump motor | Brewery cellar, night shift |
| Rigger | Clear the Hook. Lift a rooftop unit off a truck | Downtown lane at dusk |

## How to play

- Phone held sideways. Left thumb walks, right thumb inspects, drag to look all
  the way round, pinch to zoom. On Android, Start takes the game full screen and
  turns it sideways for you. On an iPhone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen, and
  open it from there to play full screen.
- Desktop. W A S D to walk, drag to look, scroll to zoom, E to inspect.
- Headset. Open the link in the headset browser and press Enter VR.

No install and no account. Nothing you type leaves your device.

## A note on the content

The hazards and procedures follow common Canadian practice. They are a teaching
aid, not a legal reference. Site rules and the current code always come first.
