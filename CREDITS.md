## Training content

The hazards, procedures and failure scenes were written for this game. They
follow common Canadian practice (Alberta OHS Code Parts 6, 10, 15, 17 and 21,
CSA W117.2, CSA Z460, CSA Z462) but they are a teaching aid, not a legal
reference. Site rules and the current code always come first.
